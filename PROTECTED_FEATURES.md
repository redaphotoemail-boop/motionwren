# Protected features — never remove, hide or gate these

Reda's standing rule: when editing one thing, change ONLY what was agreed. Never remove, hide,
move behind a condition, or rewrite anything that already works and was not part of the request.
If a request seems to conflict with an existing feature, keep the feature and ask.

Before every commit that touches `app/studio/page.tsx`, `app/studio/final-edit.tsx`,
`app/studio/edit-film.tsx`, or `app/admin/**`, run `npm run test:protected`. It fails if a
protected section below is missing. If you must change one on purpose, Reda has to approve it first.

## Studio (multi-shot film builder)
- Choice between "One prompt per scene" and "One prompt, multiple images"
- Photo upload / scene builder ("Build your scenes" / "Add your shot images") — first
- Shot type, scene timing
- "Finish your film": listing cards (house + realtor info, logo, preview), music, voiceover, sound effects — ALWAYS visible, never hidden until a photo is added
- Production settings (engine, quality, aspect, speed, continuity)
- "Make the film" (length, cost, generate, final video, download)
- History: Edit button and the Edit window (scenes & movement, cards, sound)
- Baked prompts (never touched): customer-production.ts STATIC_LOCK/MOVEMENTS; studio/page.tsx realEstateBasePrompt / realEstateMovements / realEstatePrompt / multiShotStabilityLock

## Admin
- Library Studio: video templates + MotionWren templates + music library
- Studio Ads: upload, templates, scenes, actors, sound, subtitles
- WrenCut (/admin/wrencut): reference upload/link → template "MotionWren N" (no reference info kept), History/photo preview, show-in-Studio switch, effect chips on every template thumbnail, "Update from a reference" (re-read a template)

- Edit film panel (Studio History → Edit): Scenes and movement, Listing cards, Sound, and "Your clips" (each saved clip viewable, "Edit again with a template" with the choice of which clips to use, no engine cost).

- Cinematic titles (Studio "Finish your film" → Titles, and Edit film → Titles): five fonts, words typed at the start and/or the end; drawn by the composer (composer/titles.mjs, composer/fonts); never read from a reference video.

- Saved clips (Studio, owner only): pick clips from History to make a new film with no engine charge; routes /api/studio/clips and /api/studio/production/from-clips are admin-only. History cards tag template films ("Template · name") and "Saved clips".

- Video format (Studio; one control, it replaced the separate Video style and Frame size): 16:9 Classic listing film, 9:16 Vertical property reel, 2.39 Luxury cinematic (crop-safe) and "Original · Photo shape" (the film is made at the photos' own shape, only on engines that keep it: Kling 3, Wan 3/2.7/2.5, Hailuo 2.3, H3, H3 Lite). Edit film → Frame cuts a finished film to 16:9 or vertical with no engine charge (the floor stays; composer `anchorY`).

- Verified photo upload (Studio): each photo is sent with its SHA-256, the server reads it back and replies with size + fingerprint, the start request is checked against what is stored (missing photos are re-sent), and the page says "All N photos arrived and were checked" only after that. Photos stay on the device (IndexedDB, `app/studio/pending-upload.ts`) until confirmed, with a "Send again" banner. Never show "received" without the server's receipt.

- Social media templates (Library Studio → "Social media" section; Studio template picker → "Social media"): thirteen beat-aligned templates (app/social-templates.ts) installed as ordinary templates; each shows what photos it needs in Studio and, once photos are added, a photo check (count, order, story, movement, quality — app/social-check.ts, /api/studio/social-check, owner-only) with "Arrange my photos this way"; production matches the photos to the template's shots (the recipe names each shot's photo) and keeps the template's own canvas. Existing templates are unchanged (they still get the smooth film).

- Quality loop (owner only): a flagged scene is re-made with a fix note that names the problem and the film is re-checked after each re-make — at most three attempts (`MAX_FIX_ROUNDS = 2` re-makes). History cards show the result (red "Needs attention · not fixed after N attempts" with a red outline, green "Approved" or "Fixed on attempt N · ready to deliver"); Quality → each flagged scene has "Full screen". When problems remain after the last attempt an alert notification "Quality problem not fixed · MW-xxxx" is sent; a fixed film sends "Film fixed, ready to deliver". The status comes from the film's manifest (`qualityState`), never from the browser.

- Scene-by-scene Quality (History → Quality, owner only): every scene is listed (not only flagged ones) with the number of frames the check looked at (three per scene), its attempts with the exact instruction written for each re-make (`fix.sent`, written by `app/fix-notes.ts`), full-screen playback of each attempt, and a "Fix this scene" button (price in dollars) that writes a complete instruction from the scene's recorded problems plus the camera-only rules, makes only that scene again on Wan 3.0, and saves the result as a new History version. Route: POST /api/studio/production/[id] {action:"fix-scene"} (admin only).

- Compact pickers (Studio): templates are one scrolling row with "Listing films" / "Social media" tabs; saved clips are one scrolling row per film (four films, then "Show more"), with a sticky "Make a film from these" bar. Once two or more saved clips are chosen, the main "Make film" button makes the film from them (no photos needed) and a hint says what is missing when it is grey.

- Engine reliability (Studio, owner only): a scene the engine has not started is cancelled and sent again after 6 minutes, one it is running after 4 min + 1 min per second of video (5 s: 9 min, never over 18); an engine that stalls twice on a scene stops it instead of a third wait; each stall raises a "Video engine slow" alert; the progress card shows minutes with the engine, what the engine says, and a "Send again now" button (action `restart-scene`); Stop cancels the engine job and confirms it really stopped (`cancelAndConfirm`); /api/predictions/[id] (owner) reports the engine's model, queue time and log tail.

- Make versions (Studio History → Edit film → "Make versions", owner only): tick several templates and each becomes its own new film cut from the film's saved scenes (`app/studio/variants.tsx` → /api/studio/production/from-clips, one call per template). No engine generation; a social template's canvas (9:16 or 16:9) is applied by a cut (`edit.reframe`), never a stretch.
- Requirements first (template guidance): the bold "Requirements: N photos for the best result (at least L)" line at the top of a chosen template (`template-strip.tsx`, `tpl-need`), Make film disabled below the minimum, and per-template "Needs N scenes" in Make versions (`variants.tsx`, scenes below the minimum cannot be ticked).
- Upscale is the last touch: versions from Make versions are made at the engine's own resolution (`upscale: false` in from-clips, no upscale cost). History shows "Approved? Upscale to 1080p · about $X" (`upscaleCents`, action `upscale-final` → `upscaleFinal` in render-jobs.ts), which makes a new 1080p version and keeps the approved one.
- Template shots keep their exact length (`composer/wrencut.mjs`: slow-motion look-ahead in `rampHead`, shots slowed to fit a short clip, hard length guard in `shotArgs`; `tests/shot-length.test.mjs`). A short shot made template films freeze while the music played on.
- The editor refuses a film whose picture is shorter than its music (`composer/server.mjs`, "The picture came out"); every finished film reports `build` and `videoDuration`.
- Spending guard (Admin → Finance, top of the page, `app/spend-guard.ts`, `app/api/_lib/spend-guard.ts`, `/api/admin/spend-guard`, `spend-guard-panel.tsx`): monthly and daily dollar limits, a per-film "ask me first" amount, alerts at 50/80/100%, a one-tap "Pause all spending", and an automatic stop at 100% that holds (never fails) unsent scenes, new films, re-makes, upscales and the paid AI quality check. Every engine charge passes `createPrediction`, which asks the guard last (`ProviderError` kind `budget`). Studio asks "This film costs about $X. Make it?" above the per-film amount (`confirmSpend`). Scenes already with the engine finish. The guard never takes the site down: if it cannot read its numbers it lets work continue.
- Frame sizes in Edit film (`app/studio/edit-film.tsx`): the Frame section offers the film's original size plus 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5 as tiles, several can be ticked at once and each becomes its own new History version (one edit per size, in order), the film's current size is shown as "This film now" and nothing in it looks greyed out, and the price (about 2¢ per size, no scenes made again) is shown in the section and in the footer. "Original size" goes back to the frame the film was made at. The Edit sheet keeps its warm beige look (scoped `.fin-sheet` variables in globals.css).
- Stall switch (`backupEngine` in `app/api/_lib/provider.ts`, used in `scene-queue.ts`, set in Admin → Production → Controls "If a scene stalls, use"): a scene the engine leaves "processing" past its limit is sent once to the backup engine (default H3 Lite, or Off) together with the scenes still to make; finished scenes are kept. A fast engine is caught sooner (H3 Lite 3 min, Wan 3.0 and Hailuo 2.3 6 min, H3 7 min); the film then walks down one shared order (`ENGINE_ORDER` in `app/engine-order.ts`: Wan 3.0, H3 Lite, H3, Hailuo 2.3; the first backup is the owner's choice above) and stops at the end. Client-order recovery uses the same order. Focus shift is no longer offered in the Studio movement menu (its baked prompt text is untouched).
- Replicate credit section (Admin → Production, `#credit`, `app/api/admin/replicate-credit`): an estimated balance (the amount the owner last typed minus recorded engine spend since), a credit-status line, and the Open Replicate billing button; sign-in stays with the owner.
- Engine report (`app/api/_lib/engine-report.ts`, Admin → Production "What the engine said"): when a scene stalls or fails, the engine's own status, error, log tail and timings are copied into our database before the engine deletes them (about an hour), and shown per video.
- Vidu Q3 Turbo (`vidu3turbo` in `app/engine-catalog.ts`, `app/engine-providers.ts`, `app/api/predictions/route.ts`): an optional engine in Studio and the admin engine lists; not a default and never used unless chosen. Its finished video comes from Vidu's own host (`video.cf.vidu.com`), so `isEngineVideoHost` in `app/api/_lib/engine-hosts.ts` must keep accepting `*.vidu.com`, and Vidu is handed our private photo link from the first try (it cannot read Replicate's file store).

- Production page problems (Admin → Production): "Problems" names each problem in words and lists the videos that have it, each with its start time, the time of the problem, the reason in plain words and a Fix button (`app/problem-words.ts`, `app/api/_lib/job-diagnosis.ts`, `app/api/admin/production/route.ts`); every video row shows its start time and a "History of this video" timeline with a time on every line (started, each stalled or failed try, quality re-makes, scenes made, finished or stopped, and the engine it started on). Fixes use the existing queue actions (resume, retry scene, re-join); nothing runs without a click.

- Edit film window layout: a film strip (every scene, scene count, length, engine, size) opens the window; Frame, Scenes and movement (a tile per scene with its Recreate switch), Listing cards, Titles and Sound follow as plain sections. Layout and wording only; every control stays.

- Engine label in History: a video is saved and shown under the engine it was started with (`startedEngine`, older films `fix.firstEngine`), never the engine a quality re-make or stall switch moved it to; when a scene was re-made elsewhere the card adds "Re-made on <engine>".

- Smart camera movement (Admin → Engines → Camera movement, `app/api/_lib/movement-plan.ts`, `movement-read.ts`, `composer/movement.py`): when production starts, each photo is read once (OpenCV in the composer; optionally an AI reader too) and its movement is chosen by the scene first, then the story (open by moving in, close by pulling back, no repeats). Three settings: OpenCV (default), AI reader, Off. Only the six fixed movement phrases are used and the baked prompt text is never changed; any trouble falls back to the standard order and the order never waits. The choice and its reasons are kept in the film's manifest (`movementPlan`).
- True to the photo, customer films (`app/api/_lib/true-to-photo.ts`, approved by Reda Nov 25, 2026): every customer scene pushes in a little, very slowly and eased (a push-in, a gentle arc, or at most one glide across a wide view), so the frame only ever shows less of the photo and the engine never has to invent space beyond it; the closing pull-back is the engine's push-in played backwards. Matched cuts: each scene leans or arcs the way the one before it went. The baked STATIC_LOCK is kept word for word and a frame lock is added after it. An AI reader (Cloudflare by default, Claude when `scene_reader` = claude and a key is set, `off` keeps the customer's order) names each room and the scenes play in walkthrough order (exterior, entry, living, dining, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, outdoor). Smart camera movement above still applies to Studio films. `tests/true-to-photo.test.mjs`.
- Line guard, customer films (`composer/lines.py`, `composer/lines.mjs`, approved by Reda Nov 25, 2026): in the final edit every engine scene is stabilized (a fixed 2% trim, never more), then checked against its own photo with OpenCV; a scene whose straight lines move, bend or wobble, or that shows anything beyond the photo's edges, is replaced by a still-photo move made from the photo itself. The result per scene is kept in the manifest (`lineGuardResult`). Free (ffmpeg and OpenCV in the composer). `tests/composer/line-guard.test.mjs`.
- Made once, cut to any frame (`app/original-shape.ts`, customer orders since Nov 25, 2026): customer scenes are generated in the photos' own shape and the final edit cuts them to the frame the client chose; a later change of frame is a new cut, never a new generation.

- Pipeline (`app/pipeline-steps.ts`): one numbered list of the steps every film goes through, shown in plain words on the customer homepage (five short stages, "Why it's different", `#care`) and with owner detail and on/off chips in Admin → Production ("How every film is made", `#pipeline`). Change the list in one place; keep it true to what production really does.

- Re-make spending (`autoRemake`, `AUTO_FIX_ROUNDS` in `app/fix-engine.ts`): the quality check re-makes a flagged scene on its own at most once per film, and not at all when more than half the scenes are flagged (the film is delivered and the owner alerted). Re-making by hand in History → Quality stays available. The three-attempt ceiling (`MAX_FIX_ROUNDS`) still applies to hand re-makes.

- Honest quality review (`app/api/_lib/film-review.ts`): a film is flagged, alerted and re-made only for a CONFIRMED problem: the free measured check (jumps, black frames), or something Claude's review confirms. Cloudflare's small vision model alone only leaves notes (`aiReview.notes`), because it guesses (it invented cars that were never in a photo). The review prompts ask for clearly visible problems only, "if unsure, OK", and never name an object that is not visible. Tests: `tests/film-review.test.mjs`.

- Camera smoothness check (`composer/smooth.mjs`, `shakeFlag` in `composer/qc.mjs`, `shake` in `FIX_NOTES` in render-jobs.ts): every film is measured frame by frame at its own frame rate for camera shake (how far each frame's slide strays from the smooth path, as a percent of the picture width, scene changes and fades skipped; steady films read under 0.02, shaky ones 0.024 and up). A shake over the limit is a measured problem: it flags the scene and the quality loop re-makes it with an added "perfectly smooth" instruction (still at most once, never when most scenes are flagged). Fast camera moves and template films are measured but not judged. Tests: `tests/composer/smooth.test.mjs`.

- Studio "Smart choice of movements" (`app/studio/smart-moves.tsx`, `app/api/studio/movements/route.ts`): under the scenes of a Real Estate series, a button reads the photos and proposes a camera movement per scene (same reader as customer films, per the Camera movement setting in Admin). Nothing changes until Apply is tapped; every scene's movement list stays, and the owner can change any scene by hand afterwards. It never touches the baked prompts, the Admin "last film" note, or customer films.

- Film cost breakdown (`app/cost-model.ts`, History → Cost tab, Finance, Orders, spend limits): every cost of a film is counted: engine scenes (including upscale and automatic re-makes, recorded on the job), final edit and measuring compute, music, voiceover, sound effects, the AI photo reader (own line "Camera movement reading", recorded into the film's AI cost so Spend, Finance and limits include it) and the AI quality check. OpenCV reading and the measured checks are free.

- Cloudflare costs on Finance (`app/admin/finance/cloudflare-usage.tsx`, `app/cloudflare-cost.ts`, `app/api/admin/cloudflare-usage/route.ts`): a live section with the same bars as Spending, by category (website and orders, database, photos and videos, AI, editing server), the bill so far (plan + usage beyond what it includes), refreshed every minute. Read-only; owner-only; reads Cloudflare's analytics with `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN` and `CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID` (secrets the owner adds). A category Cloudflare does not answer shows as not available and never hides the others.

- Spending bars open up (`app/admin/replicates/spend-breakdown.tsx`, `spendBreakdown` in `app/api/_lib/spend-guard.ts`, `/api/admin/spend-guard/breakdown`): Today and This month on Finance can be tapped to show what they are made of: by kind of cost (video engine, AI reading and review, editing), client orders vs tests, and film by film. The totals use the same sums as the bars (tested to agree).
- One film, line by line (`app/admin/finance/one-film-cost.tsx`, `/api/admin/film-cost`, `jobCostLines` in `app/api/_lib/film-cost.ts`): Finance lets the owner pick any film and see every cost of making it with the same bars as "What one film costs us". It shares `jobCostLines` with the Cost tab in History so both always agree.

- Editing-machine safeguards (Nov 2026 Cloudflare bill, ~800 idle machine-hours): never remove or loosen these. (1) `worker/composer.ts`: machines sleep 5 minutes after their last request, and a reply left unread for 30 minutes no longer keeps one awake. (2) Every machine call reads or lets go of its reply (`release()` in `app/api/_lib/composer.ts`); `tests/container-replies.test.mjs` runs in the build and fails it otherwise. (3) Every machine call goes through `machineStub`, which writes the machine down (`editor_machines`). (4) The watchdog (`app/api/_lib/editor-machines.ts`, from the heartbeat): every 10 minutes it knocks on machines unused for 45 minutes that are not working on a film or reading still in progress; the machine answers the knock itself, never starts, and stops only if it has really been idle 30+ minutes. (5) Once an hour, with the read-only Cloudflare token, today's real machine spend is checked against `container_daily_limit_dollars` (default $1): over it, managers are alerted and idle machines are put to sleep. (6) Finance shows real machine memory, processing and disk from Cloudflare and a watchdog line.
