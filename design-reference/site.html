Questions

Good to know.

How many photos do I need?

Seven photos make a 15-second film. Fourteen make 30 seconds, and 28 make a full minute.

Will it look like AI?

Rooms stay exactly as photographed. We add slow, steady camera moves, never new furniture or fake views.

Is it MLS-friendly?

Every video downloads in a branded and an unbranded version, so you can post on the MLS without agent details.

How long does it take?

Most films are ready in a few minutes. You get an email and a text when yours is done.

When do I pay?

You pay when you place the order, before we render. In the studio you'll see your own photos moving first, so you know exactly what you're ordering.

What if I don't like it?

Every order includes one free revision. Tell us what to change and we'll re-render it.
