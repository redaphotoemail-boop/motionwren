MotionWren

Still photos take flight. The identity, the voice, and the plan to launch.

01 · Name

A small bird.
Quick, precise, in motion.

A wren is tiny, fast, and everywhere homes are — darting through gardens and eaves. "Motion" says what the product does to a photo; "Wren" makes it light, nimble, and memorable. Two plain English words, one clean brand.

Confirmed

MotionWren · motionwren.com

Available on .com (and .ai) when checked Oct 24, 2026. Short enough to say, distinctive enough to own, and the wren gives you a logo nobody else in property video has.

02 · Mark

A wren, mid-flight, tail sweeping up.

The bird's upswept tail is the motion line — a still rising into movement. The amber sweep is the one spot of warmth, the golden-hour light that makes listings sell. It reads clean at any size and even works as the tail-sweep alone.

03 · Color

Twilight, the hour homes look best.

Listing photographers shoot exteriors at dusk because lit windows sell. The palette comes from that moment: deep sky, warm window light, clean walls.

06 · In use

The title card is the signature.

Every film, post and ad carries the same frosted card: address, price in Keylight amber, rooms in mono, with the wren in the corner. Repeat it until agents recognize it in the feed.

07 · Strategy

Win on ease, then on trust.

Competitors sell features. MotionWren sells the feeling of being done. Three choices carry the plan.

See it before you order

Agents watch their own photos moving in the studio before they pay. The live preview is the sales pitch — and you never render a film nobody bought.

Price under the leaders

PropertyVideos.ai lists $49 for one video and $220 for five. ListingVideo starts near $39 a month billed yearly. MotionWren at $39 single and $99 for five lands just under both.

Real photography DNA

Built by a working photographer. Rooms stay true to the photos, which is the trust gap AI-staging tools leave open.

Launch plan
  1. Days 1–30

    Prove it on 25 real listings

    Make free films for local agents in Worcester County and Greater Boston. Ask each for one line you can quote and permission to show the video.

  2. Days 31–60

    Publish the before-and-after

    Post three a week: the still on the left, the film on the right. Short-form vertical on Instagram and TikTok, tagged to the agent.

  3. Days 61–90

    Sell to listing photographers

    Offer a reseller plan so photographers add a film to every shoot. One photographer brings 20 agents.
